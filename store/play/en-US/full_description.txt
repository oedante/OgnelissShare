Ogneliss Share sends files between your devices directly: from your phone to your computer, from your computer to your tablet, from Android to iPhone. No cloud, no accounts and no size limits.

HOW IT WORKS
• Link your devices once: point the camera at a QR code, or send an invitation link by e-mail or in a messenger
• Then tap a device in the list and choose what to send: files, a folder, photos and videos, text or a link
• Linked devices accept files on their own — into Downloads → Ogneliss Share
• Share from any app → Ogneliss Share → the device you need

DIRECT AND FAST
• On the same Wi-Fi network files go directly, at the speed of your network
• Over the internet your devices find each other on their own — at home, at work, on the road
• Connection dropped? The transfer resumes where it stopped
• See which devices are online right now, and give them your own names

PROTECTION
• End-to-end encryption (QUIC, TLS 1.3): only the devices in the exchange can read the content
• Every device has its own key, created on the device itself
• When linking, both sides compare four digits on their screens
• A stranger can neither connect nor send you a file — such connections are rejected right away
• Every file is verified with a BLAKE3 hash
• If a direct path is impossible, data goes through a relay that only sees the encrypted stream

EXCHANGE WITH OTHER PEOPLE
• One-time exchange: a temporary connection, and the receiver confirms every transfer
• Files by link: send a link, and recipients download the files directly from your device while it is online, within 24 hours
• Any link can be revoked

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux. Desktop versions are available at ogneliss.ru/share

WHAT IT DOES NOT HAVE
• Ads and analytics
• Accounts and sign-up
• Cloud storage: your files are not kept on anyone's servers
• File size limits — as long as the receiver has enough space

OPEN SOURCE
The source code is open under the Apache 2.0 license, so everything said about protection can be verified — github.com/oedante/OgnelissShare
